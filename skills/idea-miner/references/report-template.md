# Report template

Use this structure in every format (Doc, PDF, Markdown). Keep quotes short and linked. In quick mode, the same sections, shorter.

```
# Idea Miner: <Domain> (<date>, <mode> mode)

## 1. Bottom line
3–4 sentences: how many ideas qualified, which (if any) is green, the single
biggest risk across the set, and what you'd do this week.

## 2. Idea comparison matrix
<paste score.py's table exactly>
<paste score.py's weakest-dimension lines>

## 3. Cross-platform intelligence summary
The recurring pains, grouped by cluster, with which source types confirmed each
and links. One short paragraph per cluster.

### Source coverage
| Source | Receipts used | Notes |
(every source attempted, including those that returned nothing and why)
Verification: <X of Y sampled receipts confirmed; Z dropped>.

## 4. Idea breakdowns (one per idea, in score order)
### <n>. <Idea name>: <verdict> (<total>/40)
- **Concept & pitch:** one sentence.
- **The receipt (proof of pain):** 3–5 linked short quotes / RFS / Fix My
  Itch / review gaps, each tagged with source type.
- **Graveyard autopsy & why now:** precedents (or "none found"), cause of
  death, and the specific shift that fixes it, or why it doesn't.
- **Bear case:** the critic's 2–3 strongest points.
- **Target buyer & pricing:** who signs, realistic price model; cite any
  incumbent pricing used as an anchor.
- **Scorecard rationale:** strongest and weakest dimension, one line each.
- **72-hour validation test:** a zero-code experiment with a pass/fail
  threshold (e.g. "10 DMs to <role> in <community>; pass = 3 calls booked").

## 5. Next steps
The hand-off menu: competitor deep-dive, shark stress test, both, or an
execution pack (PRD, landing page, outreach scripts, MVP architecture).
```
