# Source catalogue

Pick the rows that fit the target geography and domain and paste them into Agent 1's `{CURATED_SOURCES}` (and the graveyard list into the Historian brief). Don't send every row; a focused list gets deeper results. Reach every site by searching for it first, then fetching the result URL (see agent-briefs.md).

## Curated problem lists and investor wish-lists

| Source | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Y Combinator RFS (ycombinator.com/rfs) | Global, AI/infra, B2B | Updated each batch; archive of past seasons on the same page |
| Razorpay Fix My Itch (razorpay.com/m/fix-my-itch/) | India, all sectors | Crowd-sourced problems with Itch Scores; filter by category |
| a16z Big Ideas / Speedrun themes | Global consumer, AI, games | Annual Big Ideas memo; Speedrun publishes cohort themes |
| VC Cafe "Requests for Startups" hub (vccafe.com/requests-for-startups/) | Roundup of many funds' RFS lists | Good single entry point; links to each fund's original |
| Primary VC "Request for Startups" | US enterprise AI, fintech | Yearly post |
| Conviction outlook | AI-native applications | Thesis-style, not a list |
| South Park Commons "Requests for Curiosity" | Deep tech / AI research | Exploratory themes |
| Next Play "ideas from top investors" (nextplayso.substack.com) | Mixed, early-stage | Ideas submitted by named investors at various funds |
| Solana Foundation RFS | Crypto / onchain | Only for crypto domains |
| BigIdeasDB (bigideasdb.com) | Cross-checks RFS against complaint data | Some free research posts; database is paid |
| ProblemPool, StartupIdeasDB | India-focused problem statements | Community/AI-collected; mostly paywalled, so use as pointers and verify elsewhere |
| Smart India Hackathon problem statements (sih.gov.in) | India gov / public-sector problems | Published by ministries; good for GovTech, agri, health |

## Social and community pain sources

Reddit niche subs plus r/SomebodyMakeThis, r/AppIdeas, r/indianstartups, r/smallbusiness; Hacker News "Ask HN: what do you wish existed"; IndieHackers; X/Twitter (mostly snippet-only); LinkedIn posts (snippet-only); Quora; Upwork/Fiverr job posts (people paying humans to do a task software could do is strong pain evidence).

## Review and incumbent sources

G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Product Hunt, Google Play / App Store reviews, Shopify App Store reviews (e-commerce), AmbitionBox/Glassdoor for internal-tool pain (India/US).

## Graveyard and post-mortem sources

| Source | Notes |
|---|---|
| Loot Drop (loot-drop.io) | 1,600+ failed startups with cause of death and rebuild plans; entries may contain errors |
| Startups.RIP (startups.rip) | 1,700+ YC companies that died or were acquired, AI-written post-mortems |
| CB Insights post-mortems (cbinsights.com/research) | Curated founder/investor post-mortems |
| VCBacked Startup Graveyard (vcbacked.co/startup-graveyard) | 2,000+ closed VC-backed companies with funding data, by industry |
| Failory (failory.com) | Interviews and failure case studies |
| IdeaProof failure database (ideaproof.io/startup-failures) | Aggregated failure events, by industry |
| startupgraveyard.io | Older, small; may be offline |

AI-written post-mortems (Loot Drop, Startups.RIP) are a starting point; confirm the cause of death against a founder post, news article or Crunchbase status before relying on it.
